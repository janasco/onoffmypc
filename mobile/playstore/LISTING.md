# Play Store listing copy

## App name
OnOffMyPC

## Short description (≤ 80 characters)
Turn your PC on or off from anywhere, with live status and instant alerts.

## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)
Power your PC on or off from anywhere in the world — right from your phone.

OnOffMyPC pairs a tiny ESP32 device wired to your computer with this app, so you
can start, shut down, force-off, or reset your PC over the internet whenever you
need it. No more leaving your machine running all day just so you can reach it
later.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Power on, power off, force-off, and reset your PC remotely
• See at a glance whether each device is online and whether the PC is on
• Watch live readings — temperature, humidity, and Wi-Fi signal
• View a 7-day temperature chart and your recent command history
• Manage multiple devices from one dashboard
• Get push notifications when a device goes offline or comes back, and when a command is delivered or fails

BUILT FOR PEACE OF MIND
• Your session is stored securely on-device
• Commands are delivered reliably and replayed if your device briefly drops offline
• Instant alerts mean you always know your PC's state

HOW IT WORKS
1. Connect the OnOffMyPC ESP32 device to your PC's power and reset headers.
2. Add the device in the app and pair it.
3. Control your PC from anywhere with an internet connection.

REQUIREMENTS
You'll need an OnOffMyPC hardware device and an account. Learn more at
https://onoffmypc.com.

Questions or feedback? Reach us at https://onoffmypc.com/contact.
